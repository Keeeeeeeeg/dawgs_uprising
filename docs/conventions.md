# Dawgs Uprising (formerly Ventora Uprising): working conventions

## Several people edit this game
- The game page can be updated from more than one chat or account, often minutes apart. Before changing it, start from the latest published version, never from an older local copy. If a publish is refused because a newer version went live, merge your edits onto that version and bump past its version number.
- The artifact also publishes supporting files (music/, tex/). Republishing only the page keeps them.
- When rewriting this doc, keep the other editors' sections.
- When you replace one branch of a big builder function (e.g. one `type===` case in `unitGeo`), replace only that branch. In 22.3 a pillbox rewrite swallowed the next five branches (intc, cas, bomb, flak, sam) and planes went invisible; v22.6 restored them. After editing `unitGeo`, check that every non-struct UTYPE still builds a non-empty geometry.
- Never end a line with a `//` comment and then append code to that same line: the code becomes part of the comment. In 22.x the line `if(d.naval)return .15;` in `unitY` was swallowed this way and every ship sank to the sea floor (fixed in v22.7). It happened again in 23.70: `// at the city's scale` swallowed `const G2=flyGeo(n)...` in the city `flyer`, so every flying craft lost its hull (fixed in 23.71).

## Version number and changelog (required after every update)
- The main menu shows `GAME_VERSION` and a "What's changed" list built from `CHANGELOG`. Both sit at the top of the MENUS script (the last script).
- Every update bumps `GAME_VERSION` and adds a `CHANGELOG` entry, newest first.

## Balance philosophy (v23.86, agreed with Keegan)
- Pace: Halo Wars. Fights last long enough to pull back and bring counters; $400-a-side mirrors take about 10 s (infantry) to 20-30 s (tanks).
- Counters: strong but not absolute. The right counter wins even-money fights clearly (roughly 3 to 1, winner keeps about 40-75% of its value), the wrong unit still does some damage, and massing can brute-force. Rifles and MGs chip light vehicles (bullet pen 2 vs armour 1-3) but next to nothing on medium or heavy armour; rockets and artillery are the answer to bunkers.
- Tiers: even value per $; heavies pay in manpower and build time. Heavy Tank vs Medium at even $ should be close.
- Air vs AA: an even trade. AA beats a matching spend of aircraft but at a cost. AA ladder: Light (`qaa`) anti-helicopter, Medium (`flak`) all-round with splash, Heavy (`sam`, `mods.air` .4) anti-plane and anti-Zeppelin.
- Artillery: siege and zone denial; fragile up close, outranges every defence.
- Zeppelin: between a superunit with a real answer and a titan: it takes about 1.5-2x its cost in SAMs.
- Defences: beat about twice their cost of the units they are built against, lose to their counter (rockets, tanks, artillery). Anti-Vehicle turrets hold off about their own spec cost in tanks.
- Test harness (in /home/claude/tools, not in the game): `tune.js` (expected DPS and equal-cost Lanchester matrix from the real armour model, overrides from a JSON patch), `duel.js` (even-cost fights in the real SIM: units spawned on open ground with AI off and `orderAs(f,'amove',...)`; reports value left per side) and `def.js` (one emplacement vs a budget of attackers). Re-run them after any stat change.
- Planned (Keegan): an Empire at War style take-cover feature and garrisonable cover around the map to make infantry more viable.

## Fortress size (v23.67 to v23.70)
- The Fortress model is drawn 1.56x across (`FORT_LAYOUT.k`) and `FORT_KY` (1.44) tall; `wallKY(z)` gives a zone's height factor (`WALL_KY` 1.3 for the HQ). `BALANCE.walls.fortress` sizes already include the 1.56. Zone r 21.
- Thinner walls: `FORT_THIN={ap,t,k}` squeezes every model vertex in the wall band toward the inside (`fortThin(P,i,sc)`, `fortThinPt(a,b,k)`, both exported on `G` because the renderer calls them in `walledGeo` and `decodeFort`). `FORT_LAYOUT` walls, routes and loop are run through it at load.

## Cities and towns (v23.70 to v23.72)
- `BALANCE.cities` (`share` 1/3, `cityMult` 3, `townMult` .6, `townR` 10, `spacing` 90). Cities are picked right after the zones are made (central, contested, away from HQs, `spacing` apart) and get `z.city`. `zoneMult(z)`, `zoneValue(z)` (income, manpower and AI value) and `wallOf(z)` (cities use `walls.town`, the megawall; other towns `walls.townSmall`, the v21.6 colony town) are in the SIM; `zLabel(z)` in the renderer. City-only code tests `o.type==='town'&&o.city`.
- City FX: `CITY_FX` entries animated by `cityLOD`; `flyer(n,rx,rz,alt,w,bank,top,beam)` (one `flyHeli` per city at scale .3, with spinning `flyHeliRot`/`flyHeliTail` children and its beam scaled back up; three `flyCiv` air-cars on tight orbits; since v23.84 three `flyDrone` quad drones instead, with four `flyDroneRot` fans from `DRONE.rot`, scale .5, nose down `f.pt`). Blender scenes DU_Heli, DU_Drone.
- Manning a city wall: `cityBridges(z)` (SIM, shared with the renderer's footbridges) lists the inner tier-3 towers bridged to the wall top. `manLayout` gives a city `loop` (the wall-top road, `walkY` = `mega.h`), routes `B<ci>` (door at ground, lift core, bridge, wall top) and wall-top posts on `BALANCE.manning.cityWallFaces`. `manAt` uses only standing bridged towers (stair towers `T<i>` are the fallback) and records `u.man.route`.
- Collapse: `BALANCE.cityFall` (hp per tier + perM x height, heavyK for bombs and missiles, crush, crushR, rubble). Every splash impact on the ground calls `cityHit(x,z,r,dmg)`; at 0 hp `cityFall(i)` sets `c.gone`/`c.fallen`, frees the footprint but re-blocks the rubble core, crushes ground units nearby, throws out troops inside on that route and emits `cityFall`. Troops on the wall re-route down in `manOrders`.
- Renderer: `CITY_R[ci]` holds each building's vertex ranges in the merged per-city meshes (`CITY_PER[zid]` keeps the meshes and the bridge ranges `xVR`); `cityFallStart`/`cityFallTick` animate the drop, lean, dust, debris (`cityDeb`, four Blender debris pieces), the dust cloud and smouldering fires (`CBURN`), and raise one of four Blender rubble stumps (`cityRubble0..3`, `RUB_H`, scene DU_Rubble). Sound: `hooks.collapse` -> `Music.collapse`. Beacons, window flashes and roof searchlights of fallen towers are hidden in `cityLOD`.

## Infantry rigs (v23.76)
- Rifleman, Machine Gunner (standing `mg` and kneeling `mgk`) and Rocketeer are Blender parts (scene DU_Soldiers): `MODELS.inf_<t>_lower|torso|arms|mag` and `INF_RIGO[t]` = {torso (hips pivot), arms (shoulder pivot, from the torso), mag (centre, from the shoulders), muz (muzzle tip, from the shoulders)}, infantry frame (x1.3, then VSCALE .7). Only `lower` uses the walk shader, so it alone must keep the leg box (y < .64, .02 < |z| < .235, |x| < .45 after scaling).
- `unitGeo` returns the merged rest pose (thumbnails, menu); `makeUnitMesh` builds `mkRig` (torso and arms groups, rotation order YZX) and, for kneeling gunners, a second rig `rigK` swapped in by deploy state. `rigTick` turns the torso (55%) and arms (45%) toward `u.target`, pitches 25%/75% onto it (aircraft included), holds the low ready with no target, plays the reload (`R.rl`, from the SIM `reload` event) and the kick. The muzzle marker lives on the arms group.
- SIM: `mag`/`reload` on a unit type: `u.rounds` counts down in the fire branch and a reload sets `u.cd=reload` and emits `reload(u,T)`. Rifle `semi:true` (single shots; effects and sound `rifleShot`), MG bursts and belts, Rocketeer `mag:1`. Andrew's Sniper keeps his own prone model (`prone`, `proneGeo`).

## VTOL Transport (v23.77)
- Unit `vtol` (air, `carry` 8, `req:'helipad'`, helipad `manual`), numbers in `BALANCE.vtol`. `u.tr={ph:'go'|'down'|'ground'|'up',x,z,face,mode:'load'|'unload',us,t}`, `u.landK` (0 air to 1 landed; `unitY` lowers air units by it), `u.cargo` (unit objects). Passengers: `u.aboard`=VTOL id, `u.aboardU`, `u.stowed` (hidden, not in the spatial hash, follow the VTOL in `updateUnit`, filtered out of `orderAs`). Commands `{k:'vload',vid,ids}` and `{k:'vunload',vid,x?,z?}`; any order to a landed VTOL lifts it first (`tr.after`). Shot down in the air: everyone aboard dies (`vtolLost`); landed: they get out.
- Model scene DU_VTOL: `MODELS.vtol_body|lights|gear|nacL|nacR|rotor|ramp|gun`, pivots `VT_O`, colours `VT_ROLES`. Renderer `vtolMesh`/`vtolFrame` (nacelle tilt with speed, rotors, ramp, gear, chin gun aim, exhaust, downwash, strobes, landing light), crash `vtolHit`/`vtolImpact`/`vtolFx` with fall kind 'vtol'. Controls: infantry right-clicked onto a VTOL board it; a VTOL right-clicked onto infantry picks up the squad within 7 m; card button Unload.

## Walled bases (HQ and Fortress)
- Rules and sizes live in `BALANCE.walls`: gate HP, open radius, rebuild timers, and each base type's wall footprint in game units. `a` runs out through the gate, `b` runs across it, and `blocks` are buildings inside the walls that troops walk around. The HQ is a rectangle (`half`, `front`, `back`). The Fortress has `shape:'oct'`: a regular octagon of apothem `ap`, with a flat face toward the gate.
- SIM: wall cells are blocked in `pass`. Courtyard and gate-lane cells carry `cellZone` (zone id + 1). Each team has its own grid in `teamPass`, in which a walled zone's cells are open only if `zoneOpenTo(z, team)`: the zone is neutral, belongs to that team, or its gate is breached. `gridOf(u)` returns the unit's team grid; `orderMove` and `orderSlots` path on `landGrid(units)`.
- Any write to `pass` after setup must call `syncCell(k)` (`blockArea` and `repass` do). `repass` keeps wall cells blocked.
- `findPath` on a team grid sends a target inside a closed courtyard to the outside of that base's gate. `orderMove` only shares a group path with units that can see onto it; `followPath` re-plans when a wall blocks the next waypoint.
- The gate is a `bld` unit (`z.gateU`, `u.gateOf`). When it dies, `gateTick` sets `z.breached` and opens the courtyard to everyone. It is rebuilt after `rebuild` seconds, once the base has had no threats for `rebuildQuiet` seconds. `z.gateOpen` (0/1) is for the renderer only.
- Capture counts only ground units standing in the zone's courtyard cells. Garrison troops spawn in `z.yard`. HQ units leave by the keep door (`wall.keepDoor`), and `siloSpot` uses `wall.silo`.
- Renderer: `WALLED` holds the packed models: `hq`, `fort` and their gate leaves, `siegeBase`/`siegeHead`, `sentryBase`/`sentryHead`, `silo` and `hosp`. It also holds `anch` (upgrade mounts as `[a,y,b,yaw]`) and `dims`. `walledModel` adds the models after `bakeGroup`. TEAM/TEAMDK triangles use the zone's `teamMat`, which `paintZones` recolours. `walledDecor` replaces the old upgrade decor on walled bases, and `updateGates` animates the leaves.
- Doorways (v22.4, refitted v22.8): `WALK_DOORS` lists every point where the wall-walk loop enters or leaves a tower or gatehouse (12 on the HQ, 16 on the Fortress). `walkDoors` sizes each door to the troops (`DOOR`, true game metres, undoing `WALL_KY`) and slides it along its facing until it sits flush on the tower face, found by a ray cast against the wall model. Re-scan `WALK_DOORS` if the models or loops change.
- The Blender source is in the user's Blender session (not saved to a file). Scene "HQ Concepts" has collections HQD_Body, HQD_Gate, HQD_Protos and HQD_Upgrades. Scene "Fortress Octagon" has the current Fortress (OctBody, OctGate, OctUpgrades). Scene "Scene" holds the older square Fortress.
- Wall stairs are built in Blender with `wall_stair` (in the session's `driver_namespace['hq']`): a flight along a wall's inner face, its foot on the courtyard floor, and a landing at walk height + 0.9 that bridges the curb. Any route that units climb (`FORT_LAYOUT.routes`) must start on a passable courtyard cell (b = ±7.3 on the Fortress), never inside the wall band, or units stall at the foot.
- Test: `G.stateHash()` matches across two runs with the same seed. On every map each walled base has a courtyard and a gate, owners walk in and out, and enemies stop at the gate until it falls.
