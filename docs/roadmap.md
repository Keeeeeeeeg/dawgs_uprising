# Dawgs Uprising: art and texture roadmap (agreed with Keegan, 2026-11-18; updated 2026-11-19)

## Decisions
- Poly budget: TWO LEVELS per soldier: ~6k tris close up, ~1.5k at RTS zoom (swap by camera distance).
- Textures: PER-PART maps (1024 each: body, helmet, weapon), colour + normal baked from the detailed Blender models.
- Army colours: the current five (Dawgs green, Crimson Legion red, Iron Covenant violet, Ear Gaugers blue, Tribal Skull gold) applied in the game by mask: since 11-19 on the whole pauldron (plus knee caps and chest-plate edges), the stripe and visor lens; no camo tint.
- Rollout: the new marine REPLACES the old one straight away (no beta toggle).
- Colour Party: the bearer always holds the colours, even as the last man; only the officer fires (pistol). Done in 25.38.
- Animation set for the first in-game rifleman: everything discussed so far: low-ready idle/walk/jog, split-stance firing + reload, wide idle, kneel and prone (with transitions), deaths + flinches, cover duck, signals/acks and the idle bank, squad interactions.
- Rifle sized to the man (80%, ~0.95 m); support hand on the angled foregrip with the elbow out; arms never fully straight (reach <= ~.9).
- Pauldrons are two-piece articulated (cap with the shoulder, lower lame with the arm); vambraces taper; chest plate sides cut tight under the arms.
- (11-19) Marine body v2: ONE shared low-relief skinned shell for every role (plates are 2-4 cm bumps, edges/bolts/seams/metal in the normal + colour maps); helmet merged into it; gear (packs, pouches, racks, radio, ghillie, officer kit) as rigid pieces on chest/hip bones merged into the same mesh; twist bones in upper arm + forearm; smooth weights. Straight into the game (no render sign-off step).
- (11-19) Foot IK on terrain (feet and the kneeling knee plant on slopes), close zoom only.
- (11-19) Crowd target 1000+ soldiers: far zoom uses GPU-instanced soldiers with baked vertex-animation clips, billboard impostors at extreme zoom; close up stays skinned + IK.
- (11-19) Firing stances from Keegan's reference photos: standing = athletic split stance, knees bent, forward lean, stock high in the shoulder pocket, cheek weld, elbows down; kneeling = same upper body on one knee. Sniper: two prone variants (one leg drawn up; legs flat and splayed), bipod rifle.
- (11-19) MG team becomes THREE men: gunner (MG), tripod carrier, ammo bearer. They deploy together and do a brief teardown to move. Tripod set low; gunner prone/seated behind it, ammo bearer lying beside him feeding the belt, tripod man kneeling and spotting/pointing. Tripod man and ammo bearer are UNARMED. Deploy 2.5 s, teardown 1.5 s (no fire during either). WW2 crew photos are for vibe only.
- (11-19) Rocket team: gunner + loader with an ORIGINAL rocket-rack backpack (6 rockets nose-out, always full: visual). New reloadable launcher with a rear breech, grip, foregrip and optic; gunner kneels to fire when deployed. Loader kneels at the gunner's side out of the back-blast, turns away and ducks at each shot, turns back, slides a rocket into the breech and taps the gunner's helmet. Assisted reload ~35% faster; if the loader dies the gunner reloads alone at normal speed.
- (11-19) Army colour: the whole 'team' material (pauldrons, knee caps, chest-plate edges) takes the army colour, not just the stripe and lens.
- (11-19, v25.47) Ground vehicles move only along their hull axis (no sideways sliding / ice skating): stepToward drives along the heading (forward or reverse), kiting backs off along the hull axis, collision shoves are mostly along the hull (sideways cut to 25%). Duels unchanged within noise; fewer stuck vehicles in an all-AI test (16 vs 43 samples).
- (11-19) Player infantry default to Hold ground (no skirmishing off on their own; Aggressive in the Orders tab). Rifle squads fan out into a ragged firing line when engaging.
- Boots: longer, flatter, rounded armoured toe. Sniper: short ghillie cape to mid-back. Gear variety: packs, pouch layout, helmet extras, sleeves/patches, chest knife.

### Medium tank decisions (Keegan, 11-19)
- Role: the army's all-rounder main battle tank (good armour and gun, average speed); the light stays the fast scout/harasser.
- Gun: autoloaded 75 mm firing bursts of 3 from a ready rack, then a longer reload while the rack refills.
- Hull: T-34 style sloped hull; style mix of Panzer IV and Cold War. NO Sherman influence.
- Turret: boxy welded (flat angular plates, Panzer IV flavour) - autoloader in a rear bustle.
- Split tracks: two short track units (bogies) per side, with a notch carved out of the skirts to show the split. The heavy takes the scheme further.
